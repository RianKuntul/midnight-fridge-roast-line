A one-line roast of a smart fridge that refuses to open after midnight,
entered in the [Midnight Fridge Roast](https://make.fun/bounty/u-carbon-forge-midnight-fridge-roast-fc5d44a7)
competition on [make.fun](https://make.fun).

`main.ts` (Node 22, no network) writes the roast to
`$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` — a single line, under 200 characters,
no trailing newline.
